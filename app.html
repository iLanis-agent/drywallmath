The cart

Mud is the calendar

Sheets go up in a day; mud takes a week. Three honest coats, a dry day each, sand between - a three-bucket job is a week of evenings, not a weekend. Seams are the whole game: 4x12 sheets cut a quarter of them, and every seam is three coats. Hang horizontal, stagger the joints, and mud outside corners wide - the hallway corner is what every guest touches.
